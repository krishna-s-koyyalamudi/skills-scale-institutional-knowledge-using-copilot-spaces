# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Technical Program Managers

### Role Summary
Technical Program Managers (TPMs) coordinate complex cross-functional work, dependencies, and milestones across teams or workstreams. They connect delivery execution with product goals and operational constraints.

### Responsibilities
- Align schedules, milestones, and dependencies across multiple teams
- Track execution risks and escalate issues that affect delivery outcomes
- Translate strategy into program-level plans, sequencing, and measurable progress
- Facilitate cross-team planning, operational review, and dependency management
- Partner with Product Managers and Engineering Managers to improve delivery predictability

### Goals
- Reduce execution friction across teams
- Improve visibility into roadmap, milestones, and interdependencies
- Keep strategic initiatives aligned with delivery realities

### Typical Communication
- Cross-functional planning meetings and dependency reviews
- Milestone status updates for stakeholders and sponsors
- Weekly program health summaries and escalation communications

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works with Product Managers to translate priorities into executable milestones
- Coordinates with Project Managers to maintain program timing and communications
- Partners with Developers and Engineering Managers to validate feasibility and sequencing

---

## Engineering Managers

### Role Summary
Engineering Managers guide team delivery, technical execution, and team health. They ensure that engineering work is scoped, staffed, and delivered with quality and consistency.

### Responsibilities
- Own team-level planning, capacity, and prioritization
- Support engineering quality, delivery reliability, and technical execution
- Coach and develop engineers while managing team performance and workload
- Work with Product and Project managers to clarify scope, dependencies, and trade-offs
- Identify technical risks, process gaps, and opportunities for improvement

### Goals
- Deliver high-quality, maintainable software through predictable engineering execution
- Optimize team health, delivery throughput, and technical excellence
- Align engineering work with product and project priorities

### Typical Communication
- Team-level planning and delivery reviews
- Technical tradeoff discussions with Product Managers and stakeholders
- Weekly engineering status and capacity updates

### Interaction with existing roles
- Collaborates with Product Managers on feasibility and sequencing of roadmap items
- Supports Project Managers by providing concrete delivery estimates and risk updates
- Works with Developers to clarify implementation decisions and quality expectations

---

## Quality Assurance Leads / Test Managers

### Role Summary
QA Leads and Test Managers define quality standards, validate readiness, and ensure projects meet acceptance criteria before release. They help reduce defects and increase confidence in release quality.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy, quality gates, and release readiness criteria
- Validate user-facing workflows and critical business scenarios
- Partner with engineering on defect triage, regression planning, and test automation
- Ensure acceptance criteria are testable and measurable
- Coordinate manual and automated QA activities for milestones and releases

### Goals
- Improve confidence in feature correctness and stability
- Reduce risk of production defects and failed releases
- Ensure quality is built into delivery rather than added at the end

### Typical Communication
- QA planning sessions, test sign-off reviews, and release readiness checklists
- Bug triage discussions with engineering and product stakeholders
- Validation summaries for project reviews and stakeholder updates

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works with Product Managers to confirm that acceptance criteria are clear and observable
- Collaborates with Developers to validate fixes, regressions, and release readiness
- Supports Project Managers and TPMs by identifying quality-related risks and blockers

---

## Security / Compliance Leads

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Leads ensure that projects follow required controls, secure development practices, and policy obligations. They help teams identify and mitigate security and compliance risks early.

### Responsibilities
- Review projects for security, privacy, and compliance requirements
- Define and enforce secure implementation practices and review points
- Assess risks associated with integrations, data handling, and deployment changes
- Coordinate remediation for vulnerabilities or control issues
- Partner with stakeholders on required approvals and audit readiness

### Goals
- Reduce security and compliance risk across project lifecycles
- Prevent late-stage blockers caused by governance or policy gaps
- Support trustworthy delivery and operational readiness

### Typical Communication
- Security review meetings, risk assessments, and remediation follow-ups
- Escalation of critical issues to leadership and affected teams
- Governance and compliance check-ins during planning and release

### Interaction with existing roles
- Advises Product Managers and Project Managers on risk, controls, and delivery constraints
- Works with Engineering Managers and Developers to ensure secure implementation patterns
- Coordinates with release and operations roles to validate operational safeguards

---

## Customer Success / Adoption Leads

### Role Summary
Customer Success and Adoption Leads connect project outcomes to customer experience, adoption, and stakeholder value. They help ensure that delivery supports real usage, onboarding, and satisfaction.

### Responsibilities
- Gather customer or user feedback to inform roadmap and release decisions
- Partner on readiness, onboarding, and rollout communication for new capabilities
- Ensure product outcomes align with customer value and adoption goals
- Identify support issues, friction points, and improvement opportunities
- Share user insights with Product and delivery teams for iteration planning

### Goals
- Improve adoption, retention, and customer confidence
- Translate user feedback into actionable product and process improvements
- Strengthen the connection between delivery work and business outcomes

### Typical Communication
- Feedback reviews, adoption planning, and stakeholder updates
- Customer-facing readiness communications and release support coordination
- Cross-functional alignment with Product, PM, and support teams

### Interaction with existing roles
- Provides Product Managers with evidence on customer pain points and adoption readiness
- Supports Project Managers with milestone communication and stakeholder readiness planning
- Coordinates with Developers and QA on feature usability, issue priority, and release confidence

---

## Operations / Release Managers

### Role Summary
Operations and Release Managers coordinate deployment readiness, environment health, and release execution. They reduce operational risk by ensuring production changes are planned and managed carefully.

### Responsibilities
- Schedule deployments, release windows, and rollback decisions
- Validate operational readiness, environment health, and deployment dependencies
- Coordinate pre-release checks, smoke tests, and post-release verification
- Manage incident communication and recovery planning for release-related issues
- Partner with engineering and support teams to reduce operational disruption

### Goals
- Improve release predictability and minimize production risk
- Standardize operational readiness and rollback execution
- Support stable, observable service delivery after release

### Typical Communication
- Release planning sessions and post-deployment review meetings
- Incident coordination and status updates during live issues
- Operational readiness and maintenance communications with stakeholders

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works with Project Managers and TPMs on milestone sequencing and deployment windows
- Supports Engineering Managers and Developers in validating rollout readiness and rollback plans
- Collaborates with Security and QA leads to ensure release criteria and risk controls are met

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- The expanded role set helps teams clarify accountability, ownership, and dependencies across planning, delivery, quality, security, adoption, and release activities.
